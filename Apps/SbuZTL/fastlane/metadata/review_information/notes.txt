No account. No server of ours, no analytics, ads or tracking. The only network use is Apple's: MapKit for the map, place search and the approach route outside the bundled road graph. Vehicles, permits and places stay on the device. Voice search runs on device only.

To review:
1. First run: add a vehicle or tap "Più tardi" (Later), then follow the four-step tour.
2. Search "Duomo Milano" and tap the result: the panel shows the answer for your vehicle and moment, with its zones and sources.
3. Change the time from "Adesso" (Now) at the top: the zones and the answer change. Area C is off at weekends.
4. Tap "Calcola percorso" (Plan route), then "Avvia" (Start). This needs a location in a covered city; in the Simulator set a location in Milan.

Background modes: location and audio are used only during turn-by-turn guidance, for the position and the spoken directions with the screen locked.
