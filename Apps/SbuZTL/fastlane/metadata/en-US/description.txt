Sbu ZTL tells you whether your vehicle can enter an Italian limited traffic zone at a given time, and drives you to your destination without entering it.

The map colors the zones for your vehicle and the moment you pick.
Tap a place and read the answer: you can enter, you can enter by paying, you can't enter, or I don't know.
Every answer has its reason, source and verification date.
The vehicle calendar shows when a zone is open to you.
Declare your permits, zone by zone.
Turn-by-turn guidance has a voice and works with the screen locked.
If your destination is inside a zone, it leaves you at the edge and tells you how far to walk.

Covered cities: Milan, Florence, Bologna, Rome and Turin. Where data is missing or unverified, the app says it doesn't know.

Road signs prevail. The app doesn't replace the city's acts and doesn't guarantee you won't get a fine.

Sbu does one thing: it tells you where your vehicle can enter, and takes you there.

No subscription · No in-app purchases · No account · No ads · No tracking · Data stays on your device
