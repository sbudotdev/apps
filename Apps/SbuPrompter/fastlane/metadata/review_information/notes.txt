No account. Sbu Prompter has no server: no analytics, ads or tracking. Mac and iPhone are one app (universal purchase, same bundle id dev.sbu.prompter).

The Mac app is the teleprompter: the script scrolls in the notch, under the camera, at a fixed speed or following the voice (recognized on the Mac only), and it stays hidden from screen sharing and recordings.

The iPhone app is its remote. It is for people who read the script on the Mac while they record or present standing away from the keyboard: the iPhone starts and pauses the text, changes the speed, jumps between sections and shows the line being read and the reading time. Tap starts or pauses; swiping up and down changes the speed; swiping left and right moves between sections. Every command plays a light haptic.

The only network traffic is between the user's iPhone and Mac on the same Wi-Fi, over MultipeerConnectivity with encryption required (Local Network permission, Bonjour services _sbu-prompter._tcp and _sbu-prompter._udp). Nothing goes to the internet.

To review the remote:
1. Open Sbu Prompter on a Mac (macOS 15 or later) on the same Wi-Fi as the iPhone. It lives in the menu bar.
2. Open Sbu Prompter on the iPhone and allow Local Network. The Mac appears and joins on its own.
3. The first time, the iPhone asks for a 4-digit code: it is in the Sbu Prompter menu on the Mac ("Codice per l'iPhone"). Type it once; next times the iPhone joins without the code.
4. Tap the large area or the play button: the text starts scrolling on the Mac. Try the swipes and "Sezioni" (Sections) to jump to a part of the script.

Without a Mac nearby, the iPhone shows a single screen that explains how to connect it: the iPhone app is a companion to the Mac app, which is the same purchase, and does nothing on its own by design.

Settings on the iPhone shows the Mac in use and "Dimentica questo Mac" (Forget this Mac). On the Mac, "Dimentica gli iPhone abbinati" in the menu removes every paired iPhone.
