Sbu Prompter shows your script in the Mac's notch, right under the camera: you read and keep looking into the lens. People who see your shared screen don't see the text.

- The script in the notch, under the camera; on Macs without a notch, a pill at the top center
- Or a floating window you place and resize wherever you like
- Hidden when you share your screen, in screen recordings and in screenshots
- Scrolls at a fixed speed you can change while you read, or by voice: the text follows the words you read and stops when you stop talking
- Choose the microphone; your voice is recognized on the Mac and never leaves the computer
- Stops when you hover the mouse over the text; scroll on the trackpad to go back or forward
- Sections: a line that starts with # splits the script, and you jump from one part to another
- Countdown before you start: 3, 5 or 10 seconds
- Text color and size, background opacity, mirrored text for teleprompter glass
- A searchable script library on the Mac: drag in .txt, .md, .rtf, .docx, .odt, .html or .pdf files, or paste the text; export any script to a file
- Keyboard shortcuts that work in every app, and you can change them
- Your iPhone as a remote: start and pause, change the speed, jump between sections, see the line you're reading and the reading time

The iPhone app is the remote and it's included: it needs Sbu Prompter open on a Mac with macOS 15 or later, on the same Wi-Fi. Mac and iPhone talk only on your local network, with a 4-digit code the first time. No data goes to the internet.

Sbu Prompter doesn't record your screen or your camera: record or take the call with the app you already use.

Sbu does one thing: it lets you read your script while looking into the camera.

No subscription. No in-app purchases. No account. No ads. No tracking. Data stays on your device.
